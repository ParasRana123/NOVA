To ensure your request for sick leave is handled professionally, I have provided three templates ranging from formal to casual. Choose the one that best fits your workplace culture.
### Option 1: Formal (Best for Email/HR documentation)
**Subject:** Absence Notification - [Your Name] - [Date]
Dear [Manager's Name],
Please accept this email as notification that I am unable to attend work tomorrow, [Date], due to illness.
I plan to monitor my email periodically for any urgent matters, but otherwise, I will be offline to focus on my recovery. I anticipate being back in the office on [Date of return].
I have [mention status of any critical tasks, e.g., "rescheduled my 10:00 AM meeting"] to ensure there is minimal disruption. Thank you for your understanding.
Best regards,
[Your Name]
***
### Option 2: Short & Direct (Best for Slack, Teams, or Text)
Hi [Manager's Name],
I’m writing to let you know that I’m feeling under the weather and will need to take a sick day tomorrow, [Date].
I’ve updated my calendar and [briefly mention coverage, e.g., "asked Sarah to cover the morning check-in"]. I’ll keep you posted if my situation changes, but I hope to be back on [Date of return].
Thanks,
[Your Name]
***
### Option 3: Coverage-Focused (Best if you have urgent deadlines)
Dear [Manager's Name],
Unfortunately, I have come down with an illness and will be unable to work tomorrow, [Date].
Regarding my current projects:
*   **[Project A]:** I have uploaded the latest files to the shared folder.
*   **[Meeting B]:** I have sent a cancellation notice to the attendees.
*   **[Urgent Task]:** [Colleague's Name] has kindly offered to assist with [Task] if anything urgent arises.
I apologize for the short notice and appreciate your flexibility. I will be sure to catch up on all outstanding items as soon as I return.
Best,
[Your Name]
***
### 3 Tips for Requesting Sick Leave
1.  **Send it as soon as possible:** Sending the notice the night before is much more professional than waiting until the morning of your shift. It allows your manager time to adjust schedules.
2.  **Keep it brief:** You are not legally or professionally required to disclose specific medical details. "Under the weather" or "illness" is sufficient.
3.  **Set your Out-of-Office (OOO):** If you use Outlook or Gmail, remember to set an automatic reply and update your status on communication platforms like Slack or Teams to "Away" or "Sick."
